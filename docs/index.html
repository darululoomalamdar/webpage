About Us

Darul Uloom Alamdar is a sacred center of Islamic learning, established with the vision of nurturing faith, knowledge, and character. ...

From the very first day, our mission has been to serve the Deen of Allah (ﷻ) ...

We strive to create a balance between traditional Islamic sciences and modern education, ...

✨ Our Vision

  • To spread the light of the Qur’an and Hadith.
  • To produce Islamic scholars, teachers, and leaders rooted in Islamic values.
  • To guide the youth towards the path of righteousness.
  • To revive Islamic culture, morality, and spirituality.

📖 Our Core Values

  • ‘Ilm (Knowledge): Both sacred and worldly knowledge.
  • Iman (Faith): Strengthening belief in Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.
  • Akhlaq (Character): Good manners, respect, and humility.
  • Khidmat (Service): Serving humanity with sincerity.
  • Adab (Discipline): A lifestyle of prayer, study, and responsibility.

🕌 What We Offer

  • Hifz-ul-Qur’an (Memorization of Qur’an)
  • Tajweed and Qira’at (Correct Recitation)
  • Aalim Course (Islamic Theology & Fiqh & Dars-e-Nizami)
  • Arabic Language and Grammar
  • Hadith & Tafseer Studies
  • Moral & Spiritual Training
